Roles of Steroidal Hormones in the Development of Breast Cancer

📌 Overview

This review paper explores the relationship between steroidal hormones and the development of breast cancer. It focuses mainly on estrogen and progesterone, examining how these hormones interact with breast tissue and influence cellular growth and activity.

Steroid hormones can affect breast cells through specific hormone receptors and signaling pathways. Understanding these mechanisms may help explain the role of hormonal factors in breast cancer development and progression.

🎯 Objectives

- To understand the biological functions of steroidal hormones in breast tissue.
- To explore the roles of estrogen and progesterone in breast cancer development.
- To examine how hormone receptors influence breast cell growth and regulation.
- To review current scientific findings on hormone-related breast cancer.
- To identify research gaps and potential directions for future studies.

🔬 Topics Covered

- Introduction to steroidal hormones
- Estrogen and progesterone: synthesis and functions
- Hormone receptors and cellular signaling pathways
- Relationship between hormonal activity and breast cancer
- Hormone-dependent and hormone-independent breast cancer
- Current research findings, limitations, and future perspectives

📚 Methodology

This project is planned as a literature review based on relevant scientific articles, review papers, and research publications. Information will be collected from reliable scientific sources and organized to develop an understanding of the relationship between steroid hormones and breast cancer.

🌱 Significance

Understanding the relationship between steroid hormones and breast cancer may contribute to better knowledge of breast cancer biology, hormone-related risk factors, and the scientific basis of hormone-targeted treatments.
